## Your Rights Under Israeli Law

You have the right to:

1. **Access** - Get copies of all information we have about your child
2. **Correction** - Fix inaccurate information
3. **Deletion** - Delete your child's data (except legally required records like financial records)
4. **Data Portability** - Get data in downloadable format
5. **Object** - Object to certain uses of data
6. **Withdraw Consent** - Cancel any consent at any time

**Response time:** Within 30 days
**No cost:** Exercising your rights is free
