Everything you need to know about AI Club

AI Club is Raanana's first structured AI education program for children ages 8-18. We teach kids how to effectively use AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Midjourney in a safe, supervised, and engaging environment. Our program focuses on practical skills that prepare children for an AI-powered future.

We have three age-specific programs:

  • Young Innovators (8-10 years) - Play-based AI introduction through games and creative projects
  • Tech Explorers (11-13 years) - Programming fundamentals and AI-assisted research
  • Future Leaders (14-18 years) - Advanced applications, personal AI assistants (higher level), startup concepts, and entrepreneurship

We offer flexible program lengths:

  • 12 weeks - Foundation program covering essential AI skills
  • 24 weeks - Extended program with advanced applications
  • 36 weeks - Advanced program with portfolio development
  • 48 weeks - Complete mastery program with industry insights
