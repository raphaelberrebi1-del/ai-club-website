About AI Club

AI Club is Raanana's first structured AI education program for children ages 8-18. We teach kids how to effectively use AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, and Midjourney in a safe, supervised, and engaging environment. Our program focuses on practical skills that prepare children for an AI-powered future.
We're based in Raanana, Israel, offering in-person classes.
Classes are taught by an experienced instructor trained in both AI technology and age-appropriate teaching methods. All sessions are supervised to ensure safe and ethical AI usage.
Absolutely! Our programs are designed for all skill levels. We start with fundamentals and progress based on each age group's capabilities.

Age Groups & Programs

We have three age-specific programs:

  • Young Innovators (8-10) - Play-based AI introduction through games and creative projects
  • Tech Explorers (11-13) - Programming fundamentals and AI-assisted research
  • Future Leaders (14-18) - Advanced applications, personal AI assistants, startup concepts, and entrepreneurship

We offer flexible program lengths:

  • 12 weeks - Foundation program covering essential AI skills
  • 24 weeks - Extended program with advanced applications
  • 36 weeks - Advanced program with portfolio development
  • 48 weeks - Complete mastery program with industry insights

Students learn:

  • • Creative writing & storytelling with AI
  • • Digital art & media creation
  • • Programming & development with AI assistance
  • • Research & learning skills
  • • Problem-solving & critical thinking
  • • AI ethics & responsible usage
Students work with industry-leading tools including ChatGPT Pro, Claude Pro, Midjourney, and other age-appropriate AI platforms.
