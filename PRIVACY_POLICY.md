## Definitions

To help you understand this policy, here are key terms:

- **Personal Data:** Any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual, including name, ID number, location data, online identifier, or factors specific to physical, health, economic, social, or cultural identity.

- **Data Controller:** The entity that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data. For AI Kidz Club, this is Raphael Berrebi.

- **Data Processor:** An entity that processes personal data on behalf of the data controller (e.g., payment processors, cloud storage providers).

- **Data Subject:** The individual whose personal data is being processed (parents/guardians and students).

- **Processing:** Any operation performed on personal data, including collection, recording, organization, storage, use, disclosure, or deletion.

- **Consent:** Freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous indication of agreement to the processing of personal data.

**Physical Security:**
- Devices used to access personal data are physically secured when not in use
- Paper records (if any) stored in locked cabinets

### AI Platform Security

**Supervised Accounts:**
- Students use supervised AI accounts or shared credentials controlled by instructor

- AI accounts use strong passwords and 2FA where available

**Privacy Settings:**
- AI platforms configured with maximum privacy settings

**Student Training:**

## Data Breach Notification

We take data security seriously and have measures in place to prevent unauthorized access. However, no system is 100% secure. In the unlikely event of a data breach, we are committed to transparency and rapid response.

### What Constitutes a Data Breach?

A data breach occurs when personal data is:
- Accessed by unauthorized individuals
- Accidentally disclosed to unintended recipients
- Lost or stolen
- Altered without authorization
- Destroyed accidentally

### Our Breach Response Protocol

**1. Detection and Containment (Immediate)**
- Identify the breach and assess its scope
- Take immediate steps to contain and stop the breach
- Secure affected systems to prevent further unauthorized access

**2. Assessment (Within 24 hours)**
- Determine what personal data was affected
- Assess the severity and potential impact on data subjects
- Identify individuals whose data was compromised

**3. Notification to Privacy Protection Authority (Within 72 hours)**
- Under Israeli Privacy Protection Law (Amendment 13), we must notify the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority (PPA) within **72 hours** of becoming aware of a breach that poses a high risk to individuals' rights
- Notification includes:
  - Nature of the breach
  - Categories and approximate number of data subjects affected
  - Categories and approximate number of personal data records concerned
  - Likely consequences of the breach
  - Measures taken or proposed to address the breach

**4. Notification to Affected Individuals (Without Undue Delay)**
- If the breach poses a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify you **within 72 hours** of becoming aware of the breach
- Notification will include:
  - Description of the breach in clear, plain language
  - What personal data was affected
  - Likely consequences and potential risks
  - Measures we've taken to address the breach
  - Steps you can take to protect yourself
  - Contact information for questions and support

**How We'll Notify You:**
- **Primary method:** Email to address on file
- **Backup method:** Phone call to parent/guardian
- **Additional:** Notice posted on website (www.aikidz.club)

**5. Remediation and Prevention (Ongoing)**
- Investigate root cause of breach
- Implement additional security measures to prevent recurrence
- Review and update security protocols
- Provide updates to affected individuals as investigation progresses

### What We'll Do to Help You

If your data is compromised in a breach:

- **Free Identity Monitoring:** If applicable and available (e.g., if financial data compromised)
- **Clear Guidance:** Steps you can take to protect yourself and your child
- **Ongoing Support:** Dedicated point of contact (Raphael) for questions and concerns
- **Transparency:** Regular updates on breach investigation and remediation efforts

### Types of Breaches and Specific Responses

**Low-Risk Breach (e.g., accidental email sent to wrong parent):**
- Immediate containment (recall email if possible)
- Notification to affected individual(s) within 24 hours
- No PPA notification required

**Medium-Risk Breach (e.g., unauthorized access to attendance records):**
- Containment within 1 hour
- Notification to PPA within 72 hours
- Notification to affected individuals within 72 hours

**High-Risk Breach (e.g., financial data or sensitive student information exposed):**
- Immediate containment (within minutes)
- Notification to PPA within 24-72 hours
- Notification to affected individuals within 72 hours
- Offer identity monitoring or credit protection if applicable
- Potential law enforcement involvement

### Your Rights After a Breach

If your data is compromised:
- **Right to Information:** Full transparency about what happened
- **Right to Remediation:** We'll take steps to mitigate harm
- **Right to Compensation:** Under Israeli law, you may be entitled to compensation for damages
- **Right to Complain:** File complaint with Israeli Privacy Protection Authority

### Preventing Breaches: Our Commitment

We continuously work to prevent breaches through:
- Regular security audits and assessments
- Employee training (in this case, just me - Raphael - staying educated on best practices)
- Strong encryption and access controls
- Prompt software and security updates
- Incident response planning and drills
